Baby Steps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Baby Steps is an app for keeping track of a child's health: doctor visits, vaccinations, medications, growth, teeth, symptoms and reminders.

This policy describes what happens to the information you put into it. It is short because the app does very little with your data — by design.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your phone. Baby Steps stores your records in a database on the device and nowhere else.
  • There is no account. You do not sign up, sign in, or give us an email address.
  • We do not collect anything. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no usage tracking.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. Baby Steps has no server.
  • We never sell or share your child's health data, because we never receive it.
  • There are no ads.

What Baby Steps stores, and where

Everything you enter is written to a private database inside the app's own storage on your device. Android protects that storage so other apps cannot read it.

What is stored:

Information Why
Child's name, nickname, date of birth, sex, blood type The date of birth drives the vaccine schedule, growth charts and age display. Sex selects the correct WHO growth reference.
Country of residence It selects which national vaccination schedule the app builds.
A profile photo of your child, if you add one So you can tell profiles apart when you are tracking more than one child.
How your baby is fed, when it changed, and when solids started Asked during setup. It is context for the growth chart and for what a doctor may ask at a visit. Not shared, and not used for anything else.
Pediatrician and clinic details So they are to hand at an appointment.
Doctor visits, questions, diagnoses, notes, follow-ups The visit record.
Vaccinations: dates given, place, batch number, reactions The immunisation record.
Medications: names, dose instructions, schedules, and what was given, skipped or missed The dose log, which is what answers "did I already give it?"
Growth measurements, teeth, symptoms The health record.
Tasks and reminders The to-do list.
Photos you attach to a visit or a vaccination Prescriptions, certificates and lab results. Copied into the app's own storage.
Optional routine times (usual wake-up and bedtime) Only to suggest kinder medication times. This is a setting, not a sleep tracker.
Your acceptance of this policy and the medical disclaimer, and when So we can show you what you agreed to, and ask again if the wording changes.
App settings: your theme choice and unit preference So the app looks and reads the way you left it.

We do not receive any of it. It is on your device, in your control.

What Baby Steps does not do

  • It does not create an account or a profile on any server.
  • It does not upload, back up or synchronise your data anywhere. Android's own automatic backup to Google Drive is switched off for this app, so your child's records are not copied there either — not even by the operating system.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not contain advertising, and does not use health data for marketing.
  • It does not read your contacts, your photo library, your location or your microphone.

Permissions, and why each one exists

Baby Steps asks for as little as it can.

Permission What it is for
Notifications To show medication and vaccine reminders.
Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) So a dose reminder arrives at the dose time rather than whenever the system gets round to it.
Run at startup Reminders are cleared by a restart. Without this, restarting your phone would silently stop your reminders.
Vibrate Part of how a reminder notification is delivered. Added by the notification library the app uses.

Baby Steps does not request camera or photo-library permissions, and does not have access to your photo library.

When you attach a photo you can either pick an existing one or take a new one:

  • Choosing an existing photo opens the Android photo picker, which hands the app the single file you chose and nothing else.
  • Taking a photo opens your phone's own camera app, which returns just the photo you took. Baby Steps never sees the camera itself, which is why no camera permission is needed.

Either way, only that one file is copied into the app's own storage.

Children's data

Baby Steps holds health information about children, which both Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (No. 151 of 2020) and the GDPR treat as sensitive.

The app is used by a parent or caregiver, not by the child, and the records are entered by that adult about their own child. Because nothing leaves the device, that information is never processed by us or by anyone else.

Your data is yours

From More → Data and privacy you can, at any time and without asking anyone:

  • Export everything. Every record the app holds, as a single file you can keep or send on. The file is not encrypted once it leaves the app, so keep it somewhere you trust.
  • Delete everything. Permanently removes every record for every child. There is no copy anywhere else, so this cannot be undone and we cannot restore it for you.

You can also remove a single child's records from their profile.

Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your device.

Medical disclaimer

Baby Steps is an organiser, not a medical device and not a source of medical advice.

  • Vaccine dates come from your country's schedule as reported to the World Health Organization, and growth charts use the WHO Child Growth Standards. Both are shown with their source.
  • Reminder timing, and the suggestion of kinder dose times, are the app's own scheduling convenience — not medical guidance, and labelled as such where they appear.
  • Percentiles describe where a child sits among children of the same age. They are not a score and not a diagnosis.
  • Always follow your doctor's instructions. If you are worried about your child, contact your doctor rather than relying on this app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the app will ask you to read and accept the new version rather than quietly updating it. Your previous acceptances remain visible under More → Data and privacy.
