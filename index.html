Baby Steps

A calm, private place to keep track of a child's health — doctor visits, vaccinations, medications, growth, teeth and symptoms.

Everything stays on the phone

Baby Steps has no account, no server and no analytics. Records are stored on the device and nowhere else, and Android's own automatic backup is switched off for the app, so they are not copied to Google Drive either.

Reminders that work without a signal

Every reminder is an alarm set on the phone itself rather than a push notification from a server, so doses and vaccination dates arrive whether or not there is coverage — in a clinic basement, on a plane, or with mobile data turned off.

It organises. It does not prescribe.

Vaccination timing comes from the WHO and UNICEF Joint Reporting Form for the country you choose, not from anything the app invents. Medication schedules come from your doctor. Baby Steps stores them and reminds you, and never changes a dose, a course length or a date on its own.
